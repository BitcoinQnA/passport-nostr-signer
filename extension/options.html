Passport Prime Signer

Select which key to sign with from the extension popup (click the toolbar icon). Connect a new Passport Prime here.

Opens the system WebUSB device picker. Pick your Prime — it's the device advertising a vendor-class interface (class/subclass/protocol = 0xFF). The Nostr Signer app must be open on Prime at pair time; if no device shows up, open the app and unplug/replug.

Developer: simulator mode

Connects to a local signer binary or the KeyOS hosted simulator over WebSocket. Use this while iterating without flashing hardware. When this is off, the extension talks to your paired Passport Prime over USB.

Troubleshooting

Clears every paired USB device, remembered key selection, and site permissions — leaves the popup looking fresh. Use before a demo, after handing the device off, or if pairing gets stuck.
